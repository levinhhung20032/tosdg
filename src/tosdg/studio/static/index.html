Controls & shortcuts

Mouse — camera view

  • Drag a pink dot: pose the officer (limits are enforced). Yellow = selected joint.
  • Drag the blue square (feet): move the officer.
  • Wheel (camera view): zoom the picture around the mouse; the camera is not changed. Double-click resets.
  • Alt + click a joint (wrist, knee, elbow …): a rotation gizmo with one coloured ring per axis (X red, Y green, Z blue; a wrist has three). Drag a ring to turn the joint clockwise or back; joint limits, the wrist's elliptical range and self-collisions stop the motion. Esc, or a normal click on another joint, leaves the mode.
  • Hold left mouse on empty space: orbit the camera around the officer. Hold right mouse: look around (the officer may leave the frame).

Mouse — plan / profile views

  • Drag the blue dot (camera), yellow dot (aim / tilt), green dot (officer facing) or any joint (depth).
  • Wheel: zoom the view. Double-click: reset the zoom.

Mouse — lists

  • Right-click a background photo: delete it (asks to confirm).
  • Drop image files on the Background panel to upload.

Typical workflow

  1. Upload a background photo and drag its horizon line.
  2. Pick a camera profile, then adjust distance / height.
  3. Pose the officer (presets, or drag the dots).
  4. Check Generator check (right) and Render preview.
  5. Save to poses (photos you upload are already in the generator's background library).
